Our Identity

Inspiring Scientific Curiosity & Technical Excellence

Founded at Liverpool International Secondary College, the Science & IT Club is a vibrant student society bridging empirical science with modern software engineering and robotics.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide every student with an inclusive, stimulating environment equipped with state-of-the-art microcontrollers, robotics kits, computing workstations, and mentorship to transform creative concepts into functional prototypes.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the foremost secondary school innovation hub, fostering critical analytical thinking, team-based multidisciplinary problem solving, and technological leadership for high-impact STEM careers.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the Science & IT Club?

Any enrolled student at Liverpool International Secondary College (Grades 9 to 12) with curiosity and eagerness to learn is welcome. Prior coding or robotics experience is not required!

How do I propose my own project idea?

Simply log in with your student Google account, navigate to the "Submit Idea" page, and describe your concept. Club advisors review submissions every week and assign lab equipment.

Where do workshops take place?

Our practical workshops run every Tuesday and Friday after school (3:30 PM - 5:00 PM) in the Innovation Lab (Room 204) and the Maker Hub.
